    Bot lab

    Train a bot

    Evolves a new bot's evaluation weights through self-play: mutated variants play each other and the winners survive into the next generation.

    Training games are quick on purpose. Depth 4+ turns on the stronger search (and opening variety), at a big cost in speed.

    How the finished bot plays

    It stops at whichever comes first. Each extra move of depth takes roughly 5× longer, so time is usually the real limit.

    Leaderboard

    Top players

    Every finished game against a person or a bot moves your Elo rating. New players start at 800. Reach 1200, 1400, 1600, 1800 or 2200 with an established rating (20+ games) to earn a title: HCM, HM, HIM, HGM and Hex Legend. Some of the bots hold women's titles too (HWCM, HWM, HWIM, HWGM).

      How to play

      Chess, on a honeycomb

      Rooks & queens

      Rooks slide in six directions, through the sides of each hexagon. Queens combine rook and bishop moves.

      Three bishops

      Bishops move through the corners of each hexagon and never change color, so each side gets one bishop per color.

      Pawns

      Pawns move straight ahead and capture to the two cells forward at an angle. They can advance two from their starting cell and promote on the far edge.

      Knights & kings

      Knights jump: two cells in a straight line, then one cell turning off it, with twelve possible landing cells. Kings step one cell in any of twelve directions. There's no castling.

      Stalemate counts

      Stalemating your opponent wins ¾ of a point. Repetition, the 50-move rule and bare kings are draws.

      Making moves

      Drag a piece, or tap it and then tap where it should go. Legal cells are highlighted. During your opponent's turn you can queue up premoves; right-click or Esc clears them.
