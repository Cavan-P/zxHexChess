    How to play

    Chess, on a honeycomb

    Rooks & queens

    Rooks slide in six directions, through the sides of each hexagon. Queens combine rook and bishop moves.

    Three bishops

    Bishops move through the corners of each hexagon and never change color, so each side gets one bishop per color.

    Pawns

    Pawns move straight ahead and capture to the two cells forward at an angle. They can advance two from their starting cell and promote on the far edge.

    Knights & kings

    Knights jump: two cells in a straight line, then one cell turning off it, with twelve possible landing cells. Kings step one cell in any of twelve directions. There's no castling.

    Stalemate counts

    Stalemating your opponent wins ¾ of a point. Repetition, the 50-move rule and bare kings are draws.

    Making moves

    Drag a piece to move it. Legal destinations are highlighted while you drag. Works with a mouse or touch.
